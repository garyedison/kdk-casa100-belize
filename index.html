Dear Mr. Courtenay,

KDK Technology Ltd submits this working proposal to the Office of the Prime Minister for a one-hundred-home modular village — CASA 100 — finished-ready container dwellings on a square ¼-acre plat, each with kitchen, toilet, shower, split air and rooftop solar.

The package is sized for a government campaign, not a two-home waterfront enquiry. Factory list is dated 7 September 2026. A 10% volume discount is applied to the 100-home order. Labour man-hours and pad duration remain TBD until the first unit is timed on site. This is a working draft for review — not a contract.

Submitted mix: 30 Compact Terrace (1BR/1BA, 29 m²), 45 Hip Cottage (2BR/2BA, 54 m²), 25 Family Gable (3BR/1BA, 54 m²). Unfurnished all-in $10,541,545 USD — about $105,415 per lot — including factory modules, ocean freight China → Belize, inland delivery, campaign slabs, hurricane tie-downs, village electrical and water, package wastewater, gravel avenues, gatehouse and civic pavilion, 8% contingency and 5.5% project management. Furniture is a separate $527,500 upgrade.

We would welcome the chance to present the package at Sir Edney Cain Building at a time of your choosing.
